# Documentary basis and limitations

Sources consulted on 25 September 2026. These document positions and organizational identities, not experimentally validated prestige, competence or model perceptions. No personal office-holder titles are used in the new stimuli.

## Germany

- [Grüne Jugend, 16 December 2024](https://gruene-jugend.de/2024/12/16/eine-zukunft-zu-gewinnen/): supports removing the debt brake to permit investment.
- [Junge Liberale, 2025 program](https://julis.de/beschlusssammlung/btwp2025/): supports retaining the debt brake.
- [DIW reform discussion](https://www.diw.de/de/diw_01.c.884411.de/schuldenbremse_nicht_nur_2024_aussetzen__sondern_grundlegend_reformieren.html): supports reform to enable investment.
- [ifo, maintain the debt brake](https://www.ifo.de/pressemitteilung/2024-02-14/schuldenbremse-beibehalten), [2026 discussion](https://www.ifo.de/medienbeitrag/2026-01-30/sind-die-schuldenregeln-schuld-an-der-maroden-infrastruktur): motivation for the contrast with DIW; not a claim that ifo rejects all borrowing or investment.
- [AfD 2025 program, numbered page 54](https://www.afd.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/AfD_Bundestagswahlprogramm2025_web.pdf), [2026 parliamentary statement](https://afdbundestag.de/entwicklungsministerin-verliert-bezug-zur-realitaet/): supports retaining the debt brake. Its [alternative 2026 budget](https://afdbundestag.de/afd-bundestagsfraktion-legt-alternativhaushalt-2026-vor/) also envisages borrowing; maintaining a fiscal rule must not be equated with opposing all debt.

The historical arguments are retained verbatim despite intervening changes to fiscal policy. The comparison concerns model judgments of those presented texts, not a current factual test of German constitutional rules.

## Switzerland

- [Junge Grüne Schweiz program](https://jungegruene.ch/fileadmin/canton-filemounts/CH/Dokumente/Parteiprogramm/Parteiprogramm_Junge_Gruene_Schweiz.pdf).
- [Jungfreisinnige Schweiz climate paper](https://jungfreisinnige.ch/wp-content/uploads/sites/103/2023/04/Positionspapier_Klima_d.pdf).
- [Schweizerische Energie-Stiftung](https://energiestiftung.ch/home).
- [Avenir Suisse institutional description](https://www.avenir-suisse.ch/ueber-uns/) and [energy dossier](https://www.avenir-suisse.ch/microsite/dossier-energie/).

These sources motivate the political and specialist pairings and their differing orientations toward nuclear policy. They do not make the organizations equal in expertise, prestige, familiarity or support for every sentence of the stimuli. In particular, technology openness is not endorsement of the full historical pro-nuclear argument.
